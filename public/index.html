TOKENOMICS

How SolPot Works

100% transparent. Every bid is split on-chain. All prizes are paid instantly in $SOL (Solana), not tokens.

1

Main Jackpot — 85%

Every bid deposits 85% into the jackpot. Each bid resets the 60-second timer. Last bidder standing when the clock hits zero takes the entire pot.

2

Ranking Pool — 5%

5% accumulates in a pool distributed to the Top 10 highest-volume bidders at round end. Bid more, earn more — even if you don't win the jackpot.

3

Holder Airdrop Vault — 5%

5% goes to a daily raffle vault. Every 10,000 $SOLPOT held >24h = 1 raffle ticket. 100k+ tokens = VIP (25% bid discount + 10x tickets).

4

Buy & Burn + Platform — 5%

1% of every pot is held until it hits 0.02 SOL, then it automatically triggers an on-chain Buy & Burn of $SOLPOT. The remaining 4% goes to platform maintenance.

THE SNIPER WAR

The Golden Rule

There are no limits. The pot grows as long as players keep fighting, but the clock gets faster:

1
Decaying Timer. The first bid sets the clock to 60s. Every subsequent bid decreases the clock limit (from 60s down to a minimum of 10s). The war accelerates!
2
No Maximum Pot. The jackpot scales infinitely until someone gives up.
3
Last Bidder Wins. When the clock hits 00:00, the final bidder takes 85% of the pot.
4
Minimum 2 players. A round only settles when at least 2 unique wallets have bid. No self-wins.
5
The Golden Rule of Web3. Your transaction must be confirmed by the Solana network before the clock hits 00:00. Network lag is your responsibility. Bids confirmed after 00:00 roll over to the next round. Don't wait until the last second!
