TOKENOMICS

How SolPot Works

100% transparent. Every bid is split on-chain. All prizes are paid instantly in $SOL (Solana), not tokens.

1

Main Jackpot — 80%

80% deposits into the main pot. Each bid resets the clock. Last bidder standing when time hits zero wins the main pot.

2

Lucky Draw — 5%

Every bid is a lottery ticket. When the round settles, 5% is instantly raffled to a random participant as a consolation prize!

3

Ranking Pool — 5%

Accumulates in a pool distributed to the Top 10 highest-volume bidders. Bid more, earn more — even without winning the jackpot.

4

Holder Vault — 5%

5% goes to a daily raffle vault. Every 10,000 $SOLPOT held >24h = 1 raffle ticket. 100k+ tokens = VIP (25% discount).

5

Buy & Burn + Platform — 5%

1% triggers an on-chain Buy & Burn of $SOLPOT once reaching 0.05 SOL. The remaining 4% maintains the platform.

THE SNIPER WAR

The Golden Rule

There are no limits. The pot grows as long as players keep fighting, but the clock gets faster:

1
Decaying Timer. The first bid sets the clock to 60s. Every subsequent bid decreases the clock limit (from 60s down to a minimum of 10s). The war accelerates!
2
No Maximum Pot. The jackpot scales infinitely until someone gives up.
3
Last Bidder Wins. When the clock hits 00:00, the final bidder takes 80% of the jackpot and a lucky bidder is raffled 5%!
4
Unlimited Competitors. Any number of players can compete in the round! The clock resets on every bid.
5
The Golden Rule of Web3. Your transaction must be confirmed by the Solana network before the clock hits 00:00. Network lag is your responsibility. Bids confirmed after 00:00 roll over to the next round. Don't wait until the last second!
