🎯 1. What is SOLPOT?

SOLPOT is a high-speed Web3 auction and jackpot game built natively on the Solana blockchain, based on the highly engaging "Last Bid Wins" mechanic combined with an instant "Lucky Bidder Raffle".

How it works: Players bid SOL into a central pot. Each bid resets the countdown timer. To increase tension, the timer decays: it starts resetting to 60s, then 50s, 40s... all the way down to a brutal 10-second reset per bid. When the timer reaches zero, the last bidder wins 80% of the entire accumulated pot, while 5% is instantly raffled among all round participants as a consolation prize!

Unlike traditional opaque games, SOLPOT is 100% Auditable and Provably Fair On-Chain. Every single bid, win, and draw has a direct link to Solscan, displaying the immutable transaction hashes on the blockchain.

4. The Infinite FOMO Engine

To maximize the jackpot potential and create extreme psychological tension, the game operates on an infinite timer mechanic:

  • Decaying Resets: Every single bid placed resets the clock. To prevent endless stalling, the reset time decays as the round progresses (60s → 50s → 40s → 30s → 20s → 10s). The final battles happen in brutal 10-second increments!
  • Infinite Pot Growth: As long as players keep bidding to stay in the lead, the pot will grow continuously without a cap.
  • Last Man Standing & Consolation Prize: The round only ends when the timer hits zero without a single new bid. The last bidder standing takes 80% of the accumulated pot, and a random participant is drawn to win the 5% Lucky Pool!
  • Minimum 2 Players: To prevent self-wins, a round only settles if at least 2 unique wallets have participated. If the timer hits zero with only 1 player, it automatically extends by 60 seconds.
